A tower defense game. Currently just the scaffolding — stack not chosen yet.

## Status

Empty repo. Nothing playable yet.

## Ideas / scope

Rough notes on what this could be:

- A grid or path-based map where enemies walk from a spawn point to a goal
- Towers you place on buildable tiles, each with a range, fire rate, and damage
- Waves of enemies that escalate in count, speed, and health
- Currency earned per kill, spent on new towers and upgrades
- Lives that tick down when an enemy reaches the goal; game over at zero
