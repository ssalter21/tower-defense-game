# The decision log — October 2026

One month of [the decision log](../decision-log.md): every time the vision changed its own mind, with what it
said, what is true now, and why it moved. The other months are [August 2026](2026-08.md) and
[September 2026](2026-09.md). Entries run in the order they were written.

## 3 October 2026 — the contour comes off, and a level reads by a subtle shade of green

This reverses one point of
[the 28 September entry](2026-09.md#28-september-2026--the-board-is-smoothed-by-a-skin-and-a-level-reads-by-a-contour).
The contour was recorded there as chosen on [issue #330](https://github.com/ssalter21/tower-defense-game/issues/330),
but the answer carried no words of Sam's for it, and it shipped on
[PR #344](https://github.com/ssalter21/tower-defense-game/pull/344) on the agent's reasoning alone.

**What it said.** A level reads by a contour: a thin dark ribbon on each of the 268 edges where the level
changes, laid mid-slope, and no tint per level.

**What is true now.** No line marks a change of level. Each level's ground is a shade of the grass's own green,
the lowest level in use at 0.70 of its brightness and the highest at 1.30, an even step between; the hue never
moves, and the road, the earth, the scenery and the units are not shaded. The road pieces meet their neighbours
flush, so no line runs between two hexes either. The skin itself stands. Playtest question 7 stays, now asking
whether the shades read. Built on [issue #348](https://github.com/ssalter21/tower-defense-game/issues/348).

**Why it moved.** Sam, reading the ship frame on #344: *"I hate the contour lines, please remove them. I want
there to just be subtle shades of green between the levels."* Asked whether the smooth, sloping ground is what
he wants: *"Yes, the smooth slopes are right."* Shown two ramps of shade side by side, 0.85 to 1.15 and 0.70 to
1.30, with the steepest corridor cell cropped from each: *"B, the stronger one."* The subtle ramp showed the level
change at that cell mostly through the slope's own lighting. On the next frame: *"I still dont like the black
lines between the hexes"*. Those were the road pieces' bevelled rims, not the contour, and once each piece was
reshaped to meet its neighbours flush: *"yes, lines are gone, go ahead"*. The sheet's colour band stays
rejected: it walked blue-green to yellow-orange and tinted the road, where the shades keep one hue on the
ground alone.

The decision lives in [the vision's board section](../vision.md#the-board-is-a-maze). The record is
[ADR-0065](../adr/0065-the-ground-is-one-skin-regenerated-from-the-map.md), amended to say the same.

## 3 October 2026, later — the candidate and line frames are deleted, not kept as records

This reverses one sentence of
[the 7 September entry](2026-09.md#7-september-2026-later--the-alpha-is-signed-and-the-particle-ban-turns-out-never-to-have-been-the-rule),
asked on [issue #346](https://github.com/ssalter21/tower-defense-game/issues/346).

**What it said.** The three alpha frames stay in `docs/frames/effect-candidates/` as evidence for a decision
rather than as a description of the board. The same footing held for the rest of that folder, for
`rung-candidates/`, and for the fifteen line frames drawn from `four-lines.txt`, `pierce-lines.txt`,
`magic-lines.txt`, `creep-auras.txt` and `effects-roster.txt`.

**What is true now.** All 86 are deleted: the 36 effect candidates, the 35 rung candidates and the 15 line
frames, with the two folders' READMEs and `capture-effect-candidates.ps1` and `capture-rung-candidates.ps1`,
which drew nothing else. The decisions they were made from stay recorded in prose, in this log and on their
tickets. The pictures themselves are in the history, at [the last commit that holds them](https://github.com/ssalter21/tower-defense-game/tree/54b2c85d7d8691fccc46e4ea4d7870ccdc8cfc4d/docs/frames).

**Why it moved.** The 13 September commit that removed the candidate and set files took the fixtures their
recipes read, so none of the 86 can be redrawn, and they show the tiled board the skin replaced. The next byte
change under `content/` would have had `check-docs.ps1` refuse all 86 with nothing able to fix them. The ticket
offered three ways out — exempt them by name as records, delete them, or restore the fixtures and redraw — and
Sam's answer was *"delete them"*.
