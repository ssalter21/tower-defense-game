# The Bishop's magic bolt leaving his empty off hand instead of his mace.
#
# THE DECISION THIS DRAWS. Sam ruled on 10 Sep 2026 that the mace does not fire
# a bolt -- it is a melee weapon -- and in the same breath rejected the tome in
# every position, because it fouls the animation and the model. That settles
# what the bolt must not leave from and leaves exactly one question: where it
# leaves from instead.
#
# WHY THE OFF HAND, AND WHY IT IS NEARLY FREE. content/units.txt:470 has the
# bishop at hitscan, range 4200, damage magic -- so it is a caster and the bolt
# has to come from somewhere. Today MatchArt anchors it on Cleric_Mace, because
# an anchor follows its prop and the mace took the hand the tome used to be in.
# With the tome rejected, handslot.l is empty. A bolt from an open off hand is
# the one origin on this body that needs no prop, no new model and no second
# socket: WeaponSocket.OffHand is that bone and EffectAnchor resolves a bone
# name exactly as it resolves a node inside a prop.
#
# WHAT IT IS WORTH LOOKING AT. Moving the origin is worth 19-225 px of a
# 1,440,000-pixel frame across the four ticks a hitscan bolt lives -- so this is
# not a change a player will catch. It is a change to what the record says the
# tower is doing, which is the ground Sam put the whole Cleric question on.
# Ticks 311 to 314 are the whole life of the bolt; from 315 the two are
# pixel-identical.
#
# NOTHING THE GAME SHIPS FROM MOVES. Applied to a copy of the wired art for the
# length of one run. See UnitArtFile.cs.

label     the Bishop's bolt leaving his open off hand
question  where a magic bolt comes from once the mace is ruled out and the tome is gone
