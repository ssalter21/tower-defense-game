# from the other side of the body. The anchor line below is what actually puts
# it back on the tome.
#
# WHAT THIS COSTS. A cleric swinging a mace in his off hand is a body doing its
# own attack with the wrong arm, and every clip on this line is authored for
# the right. Whether that reads is the eye check.
