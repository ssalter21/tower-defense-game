# WHAT THIS IS FOR. Issue #280 found that every ground effect is laid at the

# disc of light hangs out over the background whenever its emitter is near a
# rim. That is only visible from a rim, and no committed defense puts an aura

#         -Width 1600

# THE THIRD TOWER IS THE CONTROL AND IS THE POINT OF IT. A frame of two clipped
# circles says nothing on its own -- what a candidate has to be judged against

# circle in a real match. So the Blessing stands at column 7 row 4, six hexes
# in from anything, and its circle should be identical under all three
# candidates. A frame where it is not is a candidate doing something nobody
# asked for.
#

# column 5 row 0 is against the top one, so the two discs overhang in different
# directions and a candidate cannot pass by being right about one axis. Both
# reach three hexes, which is six metres against a hex two metres across, so
# roughly half of each circle is off the board.
