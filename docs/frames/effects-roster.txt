# A FIXTURE ROSTER, AND NOT CONTENT. Nothing loads this except
# tools/capture-match-frames.ps1 -Units, and nothing about the game is decided
# here: content/units.txt is the roster, docs/roster.md is the design behind it,
# and this file exists for one reason only.
#
# THAT REASON: every row of content/units.txt authors no bubble at all, so
# nothing in the recorded match is ever slowed, hastened, cursed or shielded --
# and a frame of the recorded match is therefore a frame of none of that
# happening. What a slowed creep and a shielded creep look like cannot be
# photographed off a match in which neither exists.
#
# The rows below are content/units.txt with two of them changed and every other
# character identical. The ids are the shipped ids, so the recorded board, the
# recorded defense, the recorded wave and the recorded seed all play against it
# unaltered and the art binds by id exactly as it does for the real match.
#
# SO IT GOES STALE WHEN content/units.txt MOVES, and the frame beside it goes
# stale with it -- which is what check-docs.ps1's second invariant catches by
# dating every committed frame against the content it draws. The way back to
# green is to copy the roster again, put the two bubbles back on, and re-capture.
#
#   unit 1, the Minion, gains an aura: a two-hex sphere on itself, reaching its
#   own side, granting a pool worth two fifths of a health bar every thirty
#   ticks. The wave sends ten Minions at once, so most of them are standing in
#   somebody's sphere and wearing a bar.
#
#   unit 3, the Archer, gains a slow: a bubble of no radius on whatever its shot
#   hit, taking two fifths off its walking speed for ninety ticks. Four of the
#   six towers in the recorded defense are Archers, so what they are shooting at
#   is tinted.
#
# NEITHER IS A PROPOSAL. The magnitudes and durations are chosen to be visible
# in a photograph and mean nothing outside one; what the roster's real effects
# are is signed in docs/roster.md, and what any of it should look like is Sam's.
#
# The frames this produces are named effects-roster-tick-NNNN.png rather than
# match-tick-NNNN.png, because a tick of this is a tick of a match nobody can
# scrub to -- content/landmarks.txt was made from the other one.
