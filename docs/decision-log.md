# The decision log

**Where [The Vision](vision.md) records the times it changed its own mind.** A standing document that revises
itself silently is one nobody can trust the age of, so every reversal lands here — what it said, what is true
now, and why it moved — rather than being quietly edited out of the vision and forgotten. It is written, not
read: nothing an agent needs in order to act lives only here.

The entries are one file per month, so that a link to one lands on one month and not on the whole record:

| Month | Entries |
|---|---|
| [`decision-log/2026-08.md`](decision-log/2026-08.md) | 27 — the finished skeleton, the six reversals, the roster signed, the gates in and out, the fold, the collection imported |
| [`decision-log/2026-09.md`](decision-log/2026-09.md) | 31 — the vision cut to its decisions, the roster widened and signed, the transformation, the raise, the bounty, the token, the shapes, the rebaseline |

**What is *not* here:** where the vision replaces a claim in one of the five archived deep dives. That is
[the archive index](archive/README.md#what-the-vision-overturns).

## How to write an entry

- Append to the current month's file under `## <day> <Month> <year> — <what moved>`; a second entry on one
  day is `<date>, later — …`. Never rewrite an old entry: a correction is a new entry that cites it.
- Say what it said, what is true now, and why it moved, in that order.
- Cite the standing document the decision now lives in, and the ADR if one was written.
- No `### The bill`, no "what regenerated" sub-section. The commit that carries the entry holds the diff, and
  a paragraph restating it goes stale the moment the next change lands.
